Straight-up rural freight

JDH Transport runs out of Hikumutu Road, just outside Taumarunui. Justin takes on the loads the big line-haul firms don't want: farm jobs, awkward gear, tight tracks and real deadlines.

You deal with the driver, not a dispatch desk. Tell us what needs moving and when, and you'll get a straight answer on whether we can do it and what it will cost.

  • Locally owned and driven
  • One call, one clear price
