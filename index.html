About us

Kiwi owned, with the crew and gear to match

JDH Transport is a Kiwi owned and operated family business, based in Taumarunui. With a fleet of four trucks, trailers and diggers, and our own drivers and operators, a job that needs carting, shifting and digging can be sorted with one call.

Council maintenance work keeps our trucks busy day to day, and we cart machinery and take on contract work right around the country, including regular jobs in Wānaka and Queenstown. Tell us what needs doing, where and when, and you'll get a straight answer and a price.

FAQ

Before you call

Anything else, call us on 027 218 2136.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Taumarunui and work right across New Zealand, in both the North and South Islands, including regular work in Wānaka and Queenstown. Wherever the job is, call us and we'll let you know if we can do it.

How do I get a price?

Call or email with what needs moving or doing, where from and where to, and roughly when. We'll come back to you with a price.

Can you shift my digger or machinery?

Yes. We cart diggers and other machinery between jobs across the North and South Islands. Tell us the machine and its weight when you call.

Can you supply and cart metal?

We cart metal, rock and aggregate on the tippers and truck-and-trailer. Let us know the type of metal, how much you need, and whether it's supplied or carted only.

Can you help after a slip or storm?

We've had trucks and machines out clearing slips and fallen trees. Call us straight away and we'll tell you what we can do and when.

Do I need to be there on the day?

It helps to be there or have someone on site to show where things go. Make sure there's clear access for a truck and trailer.
